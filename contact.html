Frequently asked questions

What does MEPF design cover?

MEPF stands for mechanical, electrical, plumbing and firefighting. It covers the systems that make a building usable and safe: air conditioning, power and lighting, security and communication systems, water supply and drainage, and fire protection.

How long does an MEPF design take?

It depends on the size and complexity of the building and the scope of work. We agree a schedule with you at the start of every project.

Which software do you use?

We work in licensed AutoCAD, and use specialized simulation tools for lighting and electrical analysis.

Which building types do you design for?

Hospitals, hotels and resorts, educational institutes, shopping malls and theaters, commercial complexes, corporate houses, bungalows and villas, residential complexes and townships, industrial units, warehouses and pump houses, and stadiums and sports complexes.

What will we receive?

Design calculations, layouts, schematics and single-line diagrams, equipment specifications and schedules, bills of quantities and complete tender documents.
