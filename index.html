Tailor-made engineering for every project

ICG Engineering Solutions offers end-to-end MEPF design and consultancy, for private clients and government projects alike.

We approach each project with a fresh perspective and a problem-solving mindset. Every design starts with a close look at the project parameters, site context, regulations and budget, so the result balances innovation with function and cost.

We work closely with clients and architects to define requirements and align expectations, then refine the design together until it is efficient, sustainable and cost-effective.

What we design

All four disciplines from one team, from load calculations to complete tender documents, so the systems are planned together and priced accurately.

Mechanical (HVAC)

Heat load estimation, central plant design, air handling, ducting and chilled water piping.

Electrical and ELV

Power distribution, transformer and generator sizing, lighting, CCTV, access control and BMS.

Plumbing and public health

Water supply, drainage, sewage and water treatment plants, and rainwater harvesting.

Fire protection

Sprinklers, risers, fire pumps, and smoke detection and alarm integration.
