What we design

Each discipline is designed in coordination with the others, so plant rooms, shafts and ceiling voids are planned once and work on site.

Mechanical and HVAC

Air conditioning, ventilation, chilled-water plants, VRF systems, clean rooms and smoke management.

Electrical

Power distribution, lighting and controls, standby power, earthing, lightning protection and low-voltage systems.

Plumbing

Water supply, drainage, hot water, medical gas, rainwater harvesting and water treatment.

Fire protection

Sprinklers, hydrants, fire alarm and detection, fire pumps and special hazard suppression.
