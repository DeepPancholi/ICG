Mechanical and HVAC

Photo: HVAC plant room, ducting or a project drawing

We design HVAC systems that keep occupants comfortable while keeping energy use down. From central chilled-water plants to variable refrigerant flow (VRF) systems, each solution is sized for the building it serves, whether that is an office tower, a hospital operating theatre or a clean-room production line.

Our designs include energy recovery, building automation and smoke management, and are coordinated with the architectural and structural layouts in 3D.

Scope includes

  • Heating and cooling load calculations
  • Central plant design
  • Air distribution systems
  • Variable refrigerant flow (VRF)
  • Clean-room HVAC
  • Energy recovery systems
  • Building automation integration
  • Smoke management

Electrical

Photo: electrical panel room, substation or lighting scheme

We design power distribution, lighting and low-voltage systems for reliability, safety and efficiency, from switchgear and transformer sizing to lighting controls and smart building systems.

Whether it is a data centre that needs redundant UPS, a hospital that needs seamless transfer to emergency power, or a commercial building integrating solar generation, our designs are code-compliant and ready for future loads.

Scope includes

  • HT and LT power distribution
  • Lighting design and controls
  • Emergency and standby power
  • Renewable energy integration
  • Lightning protection
  • Earthing
  • UPS system design
  • Low-voltage and smart building systems

Plumbing

Photo: pump room, piping or water treatment plant

We design water supply, drainage and specialist piping systems that are reliable, hygienic and efficient over the life of the building. From domestic water to medical gas networks, each design accounts for water conservation, pressure and local regulations.

We also plan sustainable water strategies such as rainwater harvesting, greywater recycling and on-site treatment, to cut a building's water demand without compromising quality.

Scope includes

  • Domestic water supply
  • Sanitary and storm drainage
  • Hot water systems
  • Medical gas systems
  • Gas piping
  • Rainwater harvesting
  • Water treatment
  • Greywater recycling

Fire protection

Photo: sprinkler system, fire pump room or hydrant layout

We design life-safety systems that protect people, property and business continuity: sprinklers and hydrants, fire alarm networks, and special suppression systems for data centres and industrial hazards, all to the applicable local and national codes.

Every design starts with a fire risk assessment and covers smoke control, emergency lighting, fire pump sizing and a full code compliance review. We work with the fire authorities through approval and commissioning.

Scope includes

  • Automatic sprinkler systems
  • Fire hydrant systems
  • Fire alarm and detection
  • Smoke control
  • Fire pump design
  • Special hazard suppression
  • Fire risk assessment
  • Code compliance and NOC support

How we work

The same four stages on every project, so you always know what comes next and what you will receive.

  1. Brief and site study

    We understand your goals, budget and site conditions, and agree the design basis and scope.

  2. Concept design

    Preliminary layouts and system selection, coordinated early with the architect and structural engineer.

  3. Detailed design

    Construction drawings, specifications and coordinated models, checked across disciplines to avoid clashes on site.

  4. Construction support

    Site visits, contractor queries, design changes, and testing and commissioning through to handover.
