Mechanical (HVAC)

Photo: HVAC plant room, ducting or a project drawing

We design central HVAC plants and the air and water systems that serve them, starting from heat load estimation and carrying through to equipment schedules, quantities and tender documents.

Scope includes

  • Heat load estimation
  • Central HVAC plant design and equipment selection
  • Plant room and equipment layouts
  • Air handling unit (AHU) configuration
  • Duct design and pressure drop calculations
  • Chilled and condenser water piping design
  • Equipment schedules and bill of quantities
  • Tender documentation

Electrical and ELV

Photo: electrical panel room, substation or lighting scheme

We design power systems from the incoming supply down to final circuits, together with the extra-low-voltage (ELV) systems that make a building secure, connected and manageable.

Every design is backed by load analysis, short-circuit and power loss calculations, and a techno-commercial review, so equipment is sized correctly and economically.

Power and lighting

  • Load analysis and infrastructure-level design
  • High-, low- and extra-low-voltage distribution
  • Transformer and diesel generator sizing
  • Cable sizing and cabling layouts
  • Single-line diagrams (SLDs)
  • Short-circuit and power loss calculations
  • Lighting design and illumination calculations
  • Lightning protection
  • Energy management and power factor correction
  • Equipment specifications

ELV and building systems

  • Analog and IP-based communication
  • CCTV and access control
  • Building management systems (BMS)
  • Fire detection and alarm systems
  • Nurse call systems for hospitals
  • Backbone network infrastructure and cabling
  • Automation I/O lists
  • Server configuration and security integration

Plumbing and public health

Photo: pump room, piping or treatment plant

We design water supply, drainage and treatment systems that work reliably and hygienically for the life of the building, from storage tanks and pumps to fixture-level layouts.

Scope includes

  • Water supply: underground and overhead tanks, pumps and pipe sizing
  • Sewerage and drainage design
  • Sewage and water treatment plants (STP and WTP)
  • Rainwater harvesting
  • Fixture-unit based drainage calculations
  • Plumbing layouts and schematic diagrams
  • Pressure drop calculations
  • Tender documentation and detailed engineering

Fire protection

Photo: sprinkler system, fire pump room or hydrant layout

We design firefighting systems to Indian Standards (IS), with every pipe, riser and pump sized by calculation and the detection and alarm systems integrated from the start.

Scope includes

  • Sprinkler system design
  • Riser and pipe sizing
  • Fire pump design based on pressure calculations
  • Smoke detection and alarm integration
  • Design calculations
  • Complete tender packages

What you receive

Complete, coordinated documents your contractors can price and build from.

  • Design calculations

    Loads, sizing, pressure drop, short-circuit and illumination calculations for every system.

  • Drawings

    Layouts, schematics and single-line diagrams for each discipline.

  • Specifications and schedules

    Equipment specifications and schedules for procurement.

  • Tender documents

    Bills of quantities and tender packages ready to issue.

We work in licensed AutoCAD, with simulation tools for lighting and electrical analysis and an in-house library of codes and standards.

How we work

Each project is split into focused teams for design development, modeling, documentation and analysis, so every part gets the attention it needs.

  1. Strategy

    We agree the design and consultancy approach with you, and define requirements and expectations together.

  2. Analysis

    We study the project parameters, site context, applicable regulations and budget.

  3. Design development

    We refine the design with you in iterations until the solution is right for the building and the budget.

  4. Documentation

    We issue calculations, drawings, specifications and complete tender documents.
